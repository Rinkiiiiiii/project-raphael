Project Raphael is a Behavioral Analytics tool for BCBAs to use.

As it stands, BCBAs are overloaded with paper work and statistical tracking that cuts heavily into their time which could be better used on directly interacting with patients.
Their current solutions are to rely on chat bots and premade templates that provide a different set of issues without significant enough productivity increase.

For these reasons, I am building an application that aims to help to maintain, organize, and transpose data onto properly developed templates to create 
client reports and behavioral assessments for individual clients from the click of a button.

With this application, BCBAs would be able to complete their paper work in record speeds, allowing them to put more time into what matters most, their clients.
